Both removed from `site/index.html` and marked `removed_2026-07-24` in
`seen_links.csv`. **Lesson for future runs**: independent mirrors of a
listing existing is not proof it's still open — those mirrors just prove
the listing was real at some point. Job-board/search-engine indexes keep
old postings visible long after they close, so a corroboration check alone
isn't sufficient; explicit posting/end dates when findable are a much
stronger signal and should be searched for specifically, not just "is this
mentioned elsewhere."

Net effect: the "Forbes 1000 / large global employers" section is now
**empty** — flagged honestly on the page rather than left showing stale
listings.
